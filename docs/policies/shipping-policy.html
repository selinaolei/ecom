Shipping policy

Shipping is free on every order. All orders ship from Canada with a tracking number that is emailed to you as soon as your parcel is on its way.

Processing time

Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1 to 2 business days. During the holiday season we work extended hours to keep this promise.

Delivery estimates

  • Canada: 3 to 7 business days
  • United States: 5 to 10 business days
  • Rest of the world: 8 to 15 business days

Estimates start from the day your order ships. Carriers can experience delays around holidays and severe weather.

Ordering for December 1st

To open the first door on time, we recommend ordering before November 20th. Orders placed after that date will still ship right away, but delivery before December 1st cannot be guaranteed.

Shipping protection

Shipping protection can be added at checkout. It covers loss, theft or damage in transit with a free replacement, no claim forms required. Contact us through the Contact page with your order number and we will take care of it.

Address changes

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to change your address. Once a parcel has shipped we are unable to redirect it.

Customs and duties

Orders shipped outside Canada may be subject to import duties or taxes set by the destination country. These are the responsibility of the recipient.
