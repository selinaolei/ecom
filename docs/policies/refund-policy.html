30-day happiness guarantee

We want you and your dog to love the Dear Paws Advent Calendar. If you are not happy with your order for any reason, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we will make it right with a replacement or a full refund.

Eligibility

  • Requests must be made within 30 days of the delivery date shown on your tracking.
  • Items should be in their original packaging where possible. We understand that treats and toys may have been opened; tell us what happened and we will sort it out.
  • Add-ons such as shipping protection and the 30-day warranty are refunded together with the order they were purchased with.

Damaged or missing items

If your calendar arrives damaged or a door is missing its surprise, send us a photo through the Contact page within 7 days of delivery and we will ship a replacement at no cost. Orders with shipping protection are also covered for loss or theft in transit.

How to start a return or refund

  1. Contact us through the Contact page with your order number.
  2. We will confirm the next steps within 1 business day. In most cases you will not need to send anything back.
  3. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after approval.

Exceptions

Sale items, gift cards and orders older than 30 days cannot be refunded. If a refund is delayed, check with your bank or card issuer first, then contact us and we will follow up.
