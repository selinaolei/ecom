# JollyLight: things only you can do in Shopify admin

Store: 466wqt-d1.myshopify.com ("JollyLight"). Each step takes 2 to 10 minutes.

## 1. Preview, then publish the draft theme
Online Store > Themes > "JollyLight v1 draft" > Preview, then Publish. Homepage = product page (buy box 1/2/3 Curtains, add-ons, cart drawer, trust strip, Facebook posts, UGC videos, comparison, reviews, FAQ, 50% wheel).

## 2. Rename the store
Settings > General: name "JollyLight". Domain: check jollylight.store / getjollylight.com (trademark risk: "Jolly Lights" installer in SF).

## 3. Set the logo
Online Store > Customize > Header > Logo > "jollylight-logo.png" (Content > Files). Width about 130px.

## 4. Bundle prices at checkout (done, just test)
Automatic discounts: $19.95 off at 2 curtains ($149.95), $55.90 off at 3 curtains ($198.95), scoped to the curtain only.

## 6. Shipping zones (done)
Canada + Worldwide zones both have one rate: "Free Insured Shipping (6-10 business days)" at $0. Nothing to do except add the markets you want (Settings > Markets) — rates are ready.

## 7. Markets
Settings > Markets: keep Canada as primary, add United States (and International if you want). Turn on local currencies if you have Shopify Payments.

## 8. Payments
Settings > Payments: activate Shopify Payments (needs your business + bank details) and PayPal. Enable Apple Pay / Google Pay in Shopify Payments settings.

## 9. Customer email + sender
Settings > Notifications > Sender email: use the JollyLight address you want customers to see (and verify it).

## 10. Password page
Online Store > Preferences: remove the password when you are ready to launch, and paste the homepage meta description:
"JollyLight is the smart LED balcony curtain: zip-tie it to your railing, pick from 10,000+ Christmas designs in the app and light up the whole street. Free insured shipping."
