## 6. Shipping zones — DONE by connector
Canada + Worldwide zones now both have one rate: "Free Insured Shipping (6-10 business days)" at $0. The old $12 Standard and $20 Express rates were removed. Nothing to do except add the markets you want (Settings > Markets) — rates are ready.

## 7. Markets
Settings > Markets: keep Canada as primary, add United States (and International if you want). Turn on local currencies if you have Shopify Payments.

## 8. Payments
Settings > Payments: activate Shopify Payments (needs your business + bank details) and PayPal. Enable Apple Pay / Google Pay in Shopify Payments settings.

## 9. Customer email + sender
Settings > Notifications > Sender email: use the Skynap address you want customers to see (and verify it).
